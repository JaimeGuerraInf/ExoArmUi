## UPSA Format Requirements (summary)

- Page size: A4
- Margins: top/bottom 3cm, internal 3.5cm, external 2.5cm
- Headers: odd pages = chapter title, even pages = TFG title (no header on first page of chapter)
- Pagination: Roman numerals (centered, bottom) before Ch.1 — Arabic from Ch.1 (exterior position)
- No page number on cover or blank pages
- Double-sided print
- Page order: Cover → blank → Resumen+Abstract+Descriptores → blank → Índices (each on odd page) → Memory (starts on first odd page after blank after indices)
